# 🥁 Big Drum Kit

A bigger drum kit that runs in your browser: 12 drum pads plus a 16-step beat sequencer. All sounds are generated with the Web Audio API, so no audio files are needed.

## Features
- 12 drums: Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Open Hat, 3 Toms, Clap, Crash, Ride, Rim Shot, Cowbell
- Play with your keyboard or tap/click the pads
- 16-step sequencer with Play/Stop, Clear and tempo control (60-200 BPM)
- Master volume

## Keys
| Row 1 | Q Kick | W Snare | E Hi-Hat | R Open Hat |
|-------|--------|---------|----------|------------|
| Row 2 | A Tom High | S Tom Mid | D Tom Low | F Clap |
| Row 3 | Z Crash | X Ride | C Rim Shot | V Cowbell |

## How to run
Open `index.html` in your browser.

## Run online with GitHub Pages
1. Go to **Settings → Pages** in this repository.
2. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.
3. Your drum kit will be live at `https://Hanine-Ghabri.github.io/drum-code/`.

## Files
- `index.html` - the page
- `style.css` - the look
- `script.js` - drum sounds, pads and sequencer
